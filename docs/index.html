DATASET 0.0.1

200 games, replayed in your browser.

This archive contains 100 nine-player Mafia games and 100 five-player Avalon games, played on November 18–19, 2026.

Seven models take part: GPT-6.1 Terra, Claude Haiku 5.5, Gemini 3.8 Flash, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, GLM 5.3 Flash, MiMo V2.6 Flash, and MiniMax M3.1 Flash. These are recorded experiments; replaying a game makes no model requests.

Use Spectator to follow public discussion without role labels. A player perspective adds that player’s private information. Researcher reveals all roles, private events, and belief surveys.

All views follow event order. Beliefs are shown at their frozen checkpoint, even when the survey response was collected after gameplay.

Downloads contain the replay fields used here. Raw prompts, model-call logs, and operational configuration are not included in this viewer. Source archive checksums are recorded in the manifest.
