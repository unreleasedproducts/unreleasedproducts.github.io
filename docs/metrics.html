READING THE RESULTS

Definitions, samples, and uncertainty

One published snapshot

The values come directly from the paper-metrics results bundled with release 0.0.1. They cover the same 100 Mafia and 100 Avalon game IDs as the replays. The browser loads saved aggregates; no models or judges are called.

Overview counts come from the replay manifest. The source report’s survey-call counters include more calls than the accepted belief reports exported for replay, so they are not used as replay survey counts.

What the estimates mean

Most metrics pool their numerator and denominator over all eligible observations. “All models” is a pooled estimate, not the average of the seven model estimates. Intervals resample whole games, preserving the dependence between observations within each game.

Role assignments and sample sizes differ across models. Small cells and overlapping intervals do not establish a reliable ordering. Some behavioral measures are unstable with only 100 games.

Beliefs and role knowledge

Mafia detection AUC uses town observers’ final pre-vote survey each day and counts ties as half. Avalon’s Servant measures exclude Merlin and evil players, who already know some roles. Mafia top-1 role accuracy counts tied top guesses as wrong.

Faction deduction predicts good when P(good) ≥ 0.5 and excludes the observer’s own row. The trend uses villagers in Mafia and Loyal Servants in Avalon. Compare it with the always-good baseline.

Social behavior and ratings

False-accusation rates count evil speeches accusing a genuinely good player; they do not measure every kind of deception. Accusation honesty compares the target with the speaker’s highest private suspicion among living players; ties count as honest.

Solo Elo starts at 1500 with K = 32 and scale 400, averages pairwise updates, and is recentered to mean 1500. It is relative within a game type. Belief updates describe self-reports, not verified internal reasoning.
